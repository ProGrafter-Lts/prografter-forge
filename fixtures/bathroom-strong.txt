1. ROOM AND SCOPE
Bathroom measures 2.6m x 2.1m (5.5 m2). The bath is relocated to the window
wall and the WC moves to the internal wall opposite the door.

Strip-out: existing bath, WC, basin, vanity unit, all wall tiles and the
vinyl floor covering to be removed. All arisings removed from site in our own
tipper - a skip is not required and no skip cost is included.

Items supplied and installed: 1700mm steel bath with bath screen,
close-coupled WC with soft-close seat, 700mm wall-hung basin and vanity unit,
1400mm heated towel rail, and a recessed shower valve with fixed head and
handset.

Tiling: full-height wall tiling to the bath/shower wall to ceiling level,
half-height to the remaining three walls, plus the full floor area -
approximately 22 m2 of wall tiling and 5.5 m2 of floor tiling.

2. MATERIALS AND SPECIFICATION
Sanitaryware: Roca The Gap close-coupled WC and Roca The Gap 700mm basin.
Bath: Kaldewei Saniform Plus 1700 x 700.
Brassware: Hansgrohe Ecostat thermostatic concealed shower valve, with
Hansgrohe Logis basin mixer.
Tile allowance: £40 per m2 supply cost allowed for wall and floor tiles,
selected by the client from our supplier. All other materials are supplied by
us and included in the price below.

3. INSTALLATION WORKS
Plumbing: hot and cold feeds re-run in 15mm copper to the new bath and WC
positions; the soil connection is extended 900mm to the new WC position; new
isolation valves fitted to all appliances.
Electrical: new fused spur for the towel rail, four IP65-rated LED
downlights, and a new Vent-Axia extractor fan installed and wired to the
lighting circuit with run-on timer.
Waterproofing and preparation: shower and bath zones tanked using a Mapei
tanking kit over 12mm cement backer board; remaining walls over-boarded and
skimmed; floor overlaid with 18mm WBP plywood and levelling compound before
tiling.
Making good and decoration: all plasterwork made good, skirting and
architrave reinstated, and the ceiling and non-tiled walls painted in two
coats of the client's chosen colour.

4. COMPLIANCE AND CERTIFICATION
All electrical work is carried out by our NICEIC-registered electrician and a
Part P minor works electrical installation certificate will be issued on
completion.
The extractor fan achieves an intermittent extract rate of 15 l/s in
accordance with Part F of the Building Regulations, and all fittings within
zones 1 and 2 are IP-rated accordingly.
