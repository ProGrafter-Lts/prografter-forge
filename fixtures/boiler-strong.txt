1. BASIS OF QUOTATION
A site survey was carried out at the property on 6 February 2026 and a room

radiator outputs. This is a fixed price quotation, not an estimate.

2. APPLIANCE
Supply and install one Worcester Bosch Greenstar 4000 30kW combi boiler.
Rated output 30kW. The property is currently on a regular (heat only) system
with a hot water cylinder; this will be converted to a sealed combi system
and the cylinder, feed tank and expansion tank in the loft will be removed.

3. INSTALLATION WORKS
- New flue: horizontal run through the rear kitchen wall, terminating on the
  rear elevation at 2.1m above ground level, clear of the boundary and all
  openings.
- Full power flush of the heating system followed by installation of an
  Adey MagnaClean Professional 3 magnetic filter on the return, and system
  inhibitor dosed to manufacturer's instructions.
- Supply and fit a Worcester Comfort II RF wireless programmable room
  thermostat, plus thermostatic radiator valves to all bedrooms.
- Existing 11 radiators retained and rebalanced; two undersized radiators in
  the lounge replaced with type 22 panels as per the heat loss calculation.
- Gas supply upgraded to 22mm pipework from the meter to the appliance.
  Condensate to be run in 32mm pipe internally and discharged to the existing
  soil stack.
- Dust sheets and floor protection used throughout. Old boiler, cylinder,
  tanks and all packaging removed from site and disposed of.
  Making good: boiler cupboard made good; redecoration is not included.
