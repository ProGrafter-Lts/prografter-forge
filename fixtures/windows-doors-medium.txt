SCOPE
Full house window replacement plus a new front entrance door.

Good quality handles and secure locking to all opening lights.

DOORS
New front entrance door — composite door in Chartwell Green with a white internal face.
Glazed door with a glass panel to the top half.
High-security lock fitted.
