2. CONSUMER UNIT
Hager Design 10 VML metal consumer unit, 18-way, all-RCBO high-integrity
configuration, installed in the existing position in the hallway cupboard.

3. CABLING AND CIRCUITS
Cabling: 2.5mm2 6242Y twin and earth to all ring finals, 1.5mm2 6242Y to
lighting, 6mm2 to the cooker, 10mm2 to the shower, and 6mm2 3-core SWA to
the garage.
Circuits: 18 circuits in total - 4 ring finals (kitchen, ground floor,
first floor, garage), 4 lighting circuits, cooker, 10.5kW electric shower,
immersion heater, garage supply, 2 external circuits, smoke alarm circuit,
boiler supply, and 2 spare ways.
Containment: cables chased into walls to a minimum 25mm depth in oval
conduit, clipped and secured in floor voids and the loft, with surface
trunking used only in the garage.
Labelling: every circuit individually labelled and a full circuit schedule
and installation chart fixed inside the consumer unit door.
Special circuits: a 7.4kW EV charge point supply to the garage wall is
included, wired in 6mm2 SWA with its own RCBO and load-curtailment device.

4. SAFETY DEVICES
Protection: every circuit protected by its own individual 30mA Type A RCBO
in a high-integrity board arrangement.
Earthing: supply earthing arrangement is TN-C-S (PME); main protective
bonding to gas and water services renewed in 10mm2 green/yellow.
Alarms: 5 no. mains-powered interlinked Grade D alarms - 3 optical smoke
alarms to hall and landing, 1 heat alarm in the kitchen, 1 CO alarm beside
the boiler.
AFDDs: arc fault detection devices are fitted to the socket circuits serving
the bedrooms.
