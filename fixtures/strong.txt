1. SCOPE OF WORKS
Full replacement of the front driveway and adjoining side path.
Measured area: driveway 74.5 m2, side path 11.2 m2 (total 85.7 m2), taken from site measurement on 28 January 2026.

1.1 Excavation
Excavate existing tarmac driveway and sub-base to a formation depth of 300mm below finished level. All arisings removed from site to a licensed waste transfer station; waste transfer notes provided on completion.

1.2 Sub-base
Supply and lay MOT Type 1 granular sub-base, laid and compacted in two 100mm layers using a 1.5 tonne vibrating roller, to a finished consolidated depth of 200mm.

1.3 Laying course and paving
Supply and lay 30mm compacted sharp sand laying course. Supply and lay Marshalls Tegula Priora permeable concrete block paving, 60mm thick, colour Traditional, laid in a stretcher bond with a soldier course border.

1.4 Edgings
Supply and install Marshalls Keykerb KL half battered edging bedded on ST4 concrete with a haunching to both sides.

1.5 Drainage and SuDS compliance
The paving specified is permeable (Priora system) with a permeable 4/20mm clean angular stone sub-base to the driveway area, so that surface water infiltrates on site. This meets the requirements of the Town and Country Planning (General Permitted Development) Order for front garden hard surfacing; no planning application is required. No connection to the foul or combined sewer is made.

1.6 Reinstatement
Kiln dried jointing sand brushed and vibrated into all joints. Site cleared and jet washed on completion.

2. EXCLUSIONS
The following are expressly excluded from this quotation:
- Removal of unforeseen obstructions below formation level (e.g. concrete rafts, tree roots over 100mm diameter, buried oil tanks).
- Any works to the existing garage threshold or garage floor level.
- Relocation of the existing water meter chamber or any statutory undertaker apparatus.
- Electrical works, including installation of the EV charge point cabling.
- Soft landscaping, planting and turfing.
