SCOPE OF WORKS

Full strip and re-cover of the existing flat roof over the rear single-storey extension. This is a flat roof only; no pitched roof work of any kind is included in this quotation.

Strip off the existing felt covering and rotten decking. All waste removed from site by us and disposed of at a licensed transfer station.

FLAT ROOF COVERING

New Firestone RubberCover EPDM 1.14mm single-piece membrane over new 18mm OSB3 decking.

Falls: new tapered firring pieces laid to a 1:80 fall, discharging to a new outlet and downpipe at the rear right corner.

Insulation: warm deck build-up using 120mm Kingspan Thermaroof TR27 PIR board, achieving a U-value of 0.16 W/m2K, which meets the current Building Regulations Part L target for a re-covered flat roof.

Edges: membrane dressed 150mm up the house abutment and terminated under a new Code 4 lead cover flashing; GRP drip trims to the two open edges and a new parapet capping to the boundary wall side.

RAINWATER GOODS

112mm half-round black uPVC guttering to the rear extension edge.

68mm round black uPVC downpipe at the rear right corner, discharging to the existing gully.

New 16mm white uPVC fascia board and vented soffit to the extension eaves.

FLASHINGS AND WEATHERPROOFING

Code 4 lead cover flashing to the full length of the house abutment, chased in and pointed in 3:1 sand/cement.

There is no chimney to this extension roof, so no chimney flashing work is included.

There are no valleys to this flat roof, so no valley flashing work is included.

ROOF LIGHTS AND PENETRATIONS

1no. Velux CVP 100x100 flat roof window fitted centrally over the kitchen area.

New Ubbink flashing collar to the extractor duct penetration; EPDM pipe boot to the soil vent pipe.

INSULATION AND VENTILATION

Loft insulation upgrade is not included in this quotation — the extension has no loft void.

Eaves ventilation: over-fascia vent strip fitted to the extension eaves to ventilate the fascia void.

SCAFFOLD AND ACCESS

Mobile tower scaffold to the rear included in the price.

Edge protection to the rear elevation and RAMS supplied before start. Access via the side gate; the side passage to be kept clear.

MAKING GOOD

Any plasterboard disturbed internally will be re-boarded, skimmed and left ready for decoration.

Rear brickwork re-pointed where the flashing is chased in; patio and borders cleared of all debris and left as found.

BUILDING CONTROL AND GUARANTEE

The works are notifiable; they will be registered with local authority Building Control under a building notice, which we will submit on your behalf before we start, and a completion certificate will be issued to you.

15-year workmanship guarantee, backed by a 20-year insurance-backed manufacturer guarantee under the Firestone approved installer scheme.
