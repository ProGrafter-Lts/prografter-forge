1. SCOPE

Internal re-plaster after hack-off to the ground floor, board and skim to the ceilings, and external re-render to the rear and side elevations.

2. SCHEDULE OF AREAS

Lounge: 4 walls plus ceiling, 26m2.
Hall, stairs and landing: 31m2.
Bedroom 1: 3 walls, 18m2. Bedroom 2: 4 walls, 21m2.
Rear elevation render: 42m2. Side elevation render: 28m2.

Existing blown plaster hacked off back to brick in the lounge and hall; wallpaper stripped by us throughout; defective external render hacked off to the rear and side elevations. All arisings removed from site.

3. INTERNAL PLASTERING

Two-coat work: Thistle Hardwall backing coat, then a two-coat skim finish in Thistle Multi-Finish, 3mm total skim.
Ceilings: 12.5mm standard wallboard, screwed to the joists at 300mm centres; moisture-resistant 12.5mm board to the utility.
Preparation: loose material removed, cracks raked out and filled, Thistle Bond-it bonding primer to all high-suction backgrounds, hollows dubbed out, scrim tape to all board joints.
Corners and edges: galvanised angle beads to all external corners, stop beads to all window and door reveals, arch bead to the hall arch. Reveals and window returns plastered in.
Drying: allow 5-7 days before decorating. Ventilate the rooms and keep the heating low for the first week — do not force dry with heaters. Mist coat only once the plaster is fully dry (pale pink throughout).

4. EXTERNAL RENDERING

System: K-Rend K1 Scraped Texture silicone render, applied in two coats to 20mm total build-up (base coat 12mm plus topcoat 8mm).
Coats and thickness: 2 coats, base 12mm and topcoat 8mm, 20mm total.
Background preparation: elevations jet washed down, fungicidal wash applied, defective render hacked off, joints raked out and repointed, K-Rend HP12 base coat applied as the key coat with alkali-resistant mesh bedded into the background.
Insulation: render applied direct to masonry — no EWI or insulation board included in this quote.
Colour and finish: K-Rend Bath Stone (final shade to be confirmed from the sample board), scraped texture finish.
Movement joints and beading: movement joints at 6m centres and at the change of substrate, bellcast drip beads at the base of each elevation, render stop beads at the verges and abutments, external angle beads to all corners, and diagonal mesh reinforcement at every window and door corner.

5. MAKING GOOD

Protection: dust sheets and correx floor protection throughout, polythene dust barriers to all doorways, windows and frames masked, and paths, patio, planting and the driveway sheeted outside.
Existing features: coving and the lounge ceiling rose retained and worked around; skirtings and architraves removed and refitted on completion; sockets and switches loosened and plastered around; external sills, air bricks, vents, meter box and downpipes removed and refitted after rendering.
Cleanup: skip provided, all rubble and bagged waste removed from site, floors swept and vacuumed and washed off on completion.

6. STANDARDS AND WARRANTY

Internal plastering to BS 8000-10; external rendering to BS EN 13914-1. No Building Control notification required as this is a like-for-like re-render with no thermal upgrade.
K-Rend silicone render system: 15-year manufacturer system warranty, registered on completion. This is separate from our own 2-year workmanship guarantee.
