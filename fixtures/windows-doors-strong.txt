Full house window replacement together with a new front entrance door and rear French doors.

Removal: all existing timber frames, sashes and glass are taken out by us and removed from site.

2. WINDOWS
Type and style: flush casement windows throughout, with side-hung openers to all bedrooms, top-hung openers to the kitchen and bathroom, and fixed lights to the two lounge units.
Frames: aluminium-clad timber frames, anthracite grey RAL 7016 externally with a white painted finish internally (dual colour).
Glazing: double glazed sealed units, 28mm 4-20-4 make-up, argon filled, soft-coat low-E inner pane, warm-edge spacer bars. Obscure Pilkington Cotswold pattern glass to the bathroom window.
Window furniture: Yale Sparta inline espagnolette multipoint locks with key-locking cranked handles in brushed chrome, Securistyle friction stays throughout, and egress hinges with restrictors to the two rear bedroom windows.
Ventilation: Titon trickle vents fitted to all 11 new windows, providing 5,000mm² equivalent area per unit to satisfy background ventilation under Building Regulations Part F.

3. DOORS
Type and style: 1 composite front entrance door, Solidor Italia Sorrento style with a two-panel design, and 1 set of outward-opening French doors to the rear (two equal leaves, 1,500mm overall).
Materials and colour: front door is a 48mm solid timber-core composite in Chartwell Green externally with a white internal face; the French doorset is aluminium-clad timber in anthracite grey RAL 7016 externally, white internally.
Door glazing: front door glazed with a toughened obscure double glazed apron panel to the upper third; French doors glazed with 28mm 4-20-4 argon filled low-E units, laminated to the inner pane.
Door furniture: Ultion Nuclear TS007 3-star anti-snap cylinder to the front door with a Yale multipoint hook-and-deadbolt lock, brushed chrome lever/pad handle set, matching knocker and letterplate; French doors fitted with a Yale shootbolt multipoint lock, key-locking handles and flush bolts to the slave leaf.
Threshold and weatherproofing: low-level part-M compliant aluminium threshold to the front door with brush and compression seals; French doors on a weathered aluminium cill threshold with a drip bar, and both doorsets externally sealed with low-modulus silicone to the frame perimeter.

4. PERFORMANCE AND COMPLIANCE
U-value: whole-window U-value of 1.2 W/m²K for the windows and 1.3 W/m²K for the doorsets (BFRC Window Energy Rating band A+).
Certification: the installation is registered under FENSA (registration number 41827) and your FENSA certificate is issued within 21 days of completion.
Building Regulations: all replacement units meet the Part L1B thermal standard for replacement glazing, which sets a maximum whole-window U-value of 1.4 W/m²K.
Safety glazing: the following are critical locations under Part K — the two lounge windows have panes below 800mm from floor level, the front door glazed apron and the glazing within 300mm of both French door leaves are within 1500mm of floor level. Toughened glass is fitted to both low-level lounge panes and to the front door apron, and laminated glass is fitted to the French door leaves and to the adjacent side glazing at those locations.

5. INSTALLATION
Fitting method: frames fitted into the existing openings on plastic packers and fixed with stainless frame screws at 600mm centres into the reveals; insulated cavity closers to the jambs, expanding foam to the perimeter voids, and low-modulus silicone sealing internally and externally. Damp-proof course and cavity tray details retained and lapped to the frames.
Cills and trims: new white add-on uPVC cills with end caps to all windows; internal 25mm colour-matched trims and external cover trims, all silicone finished.
Making good internally: all internal reveals patched and re-skimmed in bonding and multi-finish plaster where the old frames are removed, then left ready for decoration; internal trims fitted and silicone finished.
Making good externally: mortar re-pointing to all reveals and heads where disturbed, render patching to the two rear openings, and external mastic seal to all frame perimeters.

6. FINISHES AND WASTE
Internal decoration: mist coat plus two top coats of emulsion to all replastered reveals in a colour of your choice, included in this price.
External pointing/render: pointing to all reveals is included in the price above; a £250 provisional sum is allowed for render repairs to the two rear openings, charged only if used.
Waste: all old frames, sashes, glass, packaging and offcuts are removed from site by us and disposed of at a licensed transfer station; uPVC and glass are recycled. Skip included in our price.
