1. SCOPE
Full kitchen replacement of the existing ground-floor kitchen.
Strip out the existing units, worktops, sink, flooring and appliances; all waste removed from site by us and disposed of at a licensed transfer station (skip included in our price).
Layout: partial layout change — the sink moves to the window wall and a 1.8m island is added; the oven run stays in its existing position.

2. UNITS AND WORKTOP
Unit range: Symphony Linear Gloss, Cashmere.
Carcasses: 18mm MFC carcasses with ABS edging. Doors: vinyl-wrapped MDF in matt cashmere.
Worktop: 20mm Silestone Eternal Calacatta Gold quartz to the perimeter runs and island, with a 100mm upstand.
Handles: Hafele Riva brushed brass bar handles, 160mm centres, to all doors and drawers.
Blum Blumotion soft-close hinges and Blum Tandembox soft-close drawer runners throughout.

3. APPLIANCES
- Bosch Serie 6 HBG5585S0B single oven (built-under)
- Bosch Serie 4 PIE631BB5E induction hob (integrated)
- Bosch DWB97IM50B chimney extractor hood
- Bosch SMV4HVX38G dishwasher (integrated)
- Bosch KIN86NSF0G fridge-freezer (integrated)
- Customer supplying the freestanding washing machine for the utility.
Appliance installation: all appliances above are fitted and commissioned by us.
Connections: electrical connections to the oven, hob, hood, dishwasher and fridge-freezer are carried out by our NICEIC-registered electrician; cold water supply and waste connections to the dishwasher are carried out by us; there is no gas appliance in this kitchen and the redundant gas cooker point will be capped off and certified by our Gas Safe engineer.

4. PLUMBING
Sink: Franke Sirius SID 110-50 1.5 bowl undermount sink in matt black.
Tap: Quooker Flex 3-in-1 boiling water tap, matt black.
First fix: hot and cold supply pipework re-run in 15mm copper to the new sink position on the window wall; 40mm waste re-routed to the existing soil connection. Second fix: sink, tap and all appliance connections made and pressure tested.
Dishwasher: new appliance isolation valve and standpipe/trap provided at the dishwasher position.

5. ELECTRICAL
Sockets and switches: 8 new double sockets above the worktop, 3 double sockets to the island, and 2 switched fused spurs to the left of the sink.
Under-cabinet lighting: Sensio Nemo LED strip lighting to all wall-unit runs, plus plinth lighting to the island, on a dimmable driver.
Consumer unit: existing Wylex 12-way board inspected — 4 spare ways available and confirmed capable of taking the two new circuits below without a board upgrade.
Circuits: new dedicated 32A radial circuit for the hob and a new 20A radial for the oven, both RCBO-protected.

6. FLOORING AND FINISHES
Flooring: Karndean Van Gogh VGW81T Hallowed Oak LVT, fully bonded over a 3mm latex screed.
Splashback: 6mm toughened low-iron glass splashback behind the hob, colour-matched to the doors.
Wall tiling: 9 m2 of 300x100 metro tiling between the worktop and the wall units on the sink and oven runs.

7. MAKING GOOD
Making good: patch and repair plasterboard where the old wall units are removed, fill all chases from the new socket and lighting circuits, and make good around the new socket positions.
Plastering: full re-skim of the sink wall and the ceiling to the kitchen, plus patch plaster to the oven run.
Decoration: mist coat plus two topcoats in Dulux Trade Vinyl Matt to the kitchen walls and ceiling.

8. CERTIFICATION
Electrical: all works notified under Part P by our NICEIC-registered electrician; an Electrical Installation Certificate and Building Control compliance certificate will be issued on completion.
Gas: capping of the redundant gas point carried out by our Gas Safe registered engineer, registration number 512884, with a Gas Safety Record issued on completion.
